challenge: 'Most project tools assume a straight line: a start, a plan, a delivery. Community projects rarely work that way. People join and leave, ideas are set aside and picked up again, and what one cycle produces becomes the start of the next.'
approach: 'The method, the interloop, treats time as a spiral. It has three vectors of action, sowing, handling and regeneration, and five base elements that the community defines: seed, soil, seedling, fruits and silo. The commposter, across all three vectors, is the place to set things aside and return to them later. The cycle can start from any point, and each vector has its own set of prompt cards. The vocabulary comes from plants, gardening and permaculture, so that it reaches across contexts.'
outcome: Version 0.1 was drafted in workshops during Tropixel Sementes 2022 at Instituto Neos, on the northern coast of Ubatuba. Version 0.2 followed in 2023, and version 0.3, the current one, in 2024–25, with the interloop, the prompt cards and open documentation on GitHub. In February 2025, a two-week residency hosted by Gaiato in Ipiranguinha, Ubatuba, evolved and tested a streamlined version of the method. In April 2026, I used SEED to frame the participatory process in my plans for Escola da Costa. The work was funded mainly through grants in partnership with the University of Bristol (Newton Fund, BDFI, ISPF).
