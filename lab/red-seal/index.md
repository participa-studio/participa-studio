challenge: The boat came with a name, SEAL, and a history. I wanted a crest that would carry my own north–south tension without erasing what the boat already was.
approach: 'The first idea was the Bear and the Wolf: the polar bear for the north, the lobo-guará (maned wolf) for the south. Out of respect for the boat''s original name and its legacy, the idea moved to Red Seal: red for the lobo-guará, Seal for the legacy. Around the crest grew a myth, written in three versions and recorded as an audiobook in English and Portuguese.'
outcome: 'The crest now lives on the boat''s burgees, plates, stickers and beanies, and the myth exists as text and audio. Together they show how I work when no one sets the brief: a personal story turned into an identity, a narrative and things people can hold.'
