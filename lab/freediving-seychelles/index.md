challenge: 'Sophie Walsh grew up diving along the coasts of the Seychelles and became an AIDA freediving instructor. She was starting her own school: a local business teaching people to freedive, with an education drive that reaches children and teens, who learn water confidence alongside the value of a healthy ocean, from plastic pollution to overfishing. She asked for a brand that could carry the business, the teaching and the activism.'
approach: I was training freediving with her at the time. We worked it out together over email and WhatsApp, in a long and rich exchange, until we reached a brand she recognised as hers. I then simulated how it would live in use, on shirts, flip-flops and other applications.
outcome: Sophie loved the result and wrote several thank-you notes about it. The logo is still in use on the school's website, five years on. Design given as support to a small local business with an education and environmental purpose, made with her rather than for her.
