challenge: 'The standard business model canvas centres on return on investment (ROI): revenue from paying customers is the outcome that counts. A social enterprise is not a non-profit, so it needs revenue too, but it often serves people who do not pay, creates value that is not revenue, and has to account for the non-human parties its work affects, such as nature and ecosystems. The standard canvas has no place for any of these.'
approach: 'The canvas adapts the Social Business Model Canvas and borrows Externalities from the SE Canvas. Its fields: key partnerships, key activities, key people and resources, cost structure, a value proposition split into social and regenerative value, relationships, channels, key metrics, financial sustainability, beneficiaries and customers as separate segments, externalities (positive, negative, PESTEL), surplus and funding. The aim is one visual overview that holds social, environmental and financial value side by side, including the effects the enterprise has outside itself.'
outcome: 'I published a first version as a free template on Miroverse, where it has been used 52 times and viewed 1,100 times (November 2026). In 2025 I piloted it in a one-to-one ideation session, exploring an enterprise in which decentralised Indigenous communities in the Amazon would share knowledge, grow seedlings and store seeds of native species locally, under a shared brand. In September 2026 I used it again to draft the first concept and business plan of ReFloat.'
