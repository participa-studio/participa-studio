challenge: 'URBiNAT works with citizens of social housing neighbourhoods to regenerate them with nature-based solutions (NBS), chosen and co-created from a shared catalogue. In 2023 the brief was a healthy corridor in Eiras, on the edge of Coimbra, linking the social housing neighbourhoods of Relvinha, Ingote and Monte Formoso with the Choupal natural park. Each group took one area. In Ingote, the question was how such solutions could also open social development pathways for the people who live there.'
approach: 'At the CASA-URBiNAT Healthy Home Summer School (University of Coimbra, 6 to 12 July 2023), part of the EU Horizon 2020 project URBiNAT, I was the designer of the Ingote group and facilitated its sessions. Three days of lectures, visits and local diagnosis led to three days of open studio co-design, at the pace of a startup weekend. While my colleagues, architects, led the spatial assessments, I worked out the co-creation protocols with the team and then facilitated them with children from the local primary school. In the square, I asked the children to imagine how it would look if they could redesign everything. We brainstormed, acted and adjusted the research as the children responded. Using the URBiNAT methodology and its NBS catalogue, we also took part in co-diagnosis fieldwork with researchers and residents, mapping the places where community ownership could grow.'
outcome: 'INGOSA proposed to take a project the school had already run with the national programme Bairros Saudáveis to other associations in the public space of Ingote. The group also proposed three new nature-based solutions for the URBiNAT catalogue. For me, it was first-hand confirmation that participatory design could serve social impact work with communities.'
